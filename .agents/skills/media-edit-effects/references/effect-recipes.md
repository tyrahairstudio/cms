# Effect recipes

Use one recipe as the visual anchor. Adapt intensity to the source instead of stacking every effect.

## Premium salon

- Warm-neutral white balance
- Soft highlight roll-off and moderate contrast
- Accurate skin and hair color
- Subtle vignette or edge darkening only when it improves focus
- Clean cuts or short dissolves; no novelty transitions

## Beauty editorial

- Crisp hair texture and controlled specular highlights
- Slightly deeper blacks without losing strand detail
- Slow push-in, speed ramp, or detail close-up used once for emphasis
- Minimal typography with generous spacing

## Bright social

- Clear subject in the first frame
- Slightly stronger saturation and midtone contrast
- Fast cuts synchronized to meaningful audio beats
- Captions inside mobile safe areas

## Before and after

- Match framing, scale, exposure, and white balance across both views
- Use a clean split, wipe, or matched cut
- Do not alter the “before” image in a misleading way
- Hold the “after” long enough to inspect the result

## Website hero

- Calm motion, 6–12 second loop, and no abrupt first/last frame
- Avoid embedded text unless the page cannot supply accessible HTML text
- Export a poster frame and a compressed web video when useful
