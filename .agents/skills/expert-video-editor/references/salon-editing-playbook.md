# Salon editing playbook

## Transformation reel: 15–30 seconds

1. `0:00–0:02` — Lead with the finished look or a fast before/after contrast.
2. `0:02–0:06` — Establish the before state without unflattering manipulation.
3. `0:06–0:15` — Show two to four meaningful process details: consultation, sectioning, color, cutting, styling.
4. `0:15–0:24` — Reveal the result with a clean match cut, turn, or hair movement.
5. Final 2–3 seconds — Hold the best result and add a short booking CTA.

## Service spotlight: 20–45 seconds

- Open with the client benefit, not a generic logo animation.
- Show tools and process only when they build trust or explain quality.
- Mix wide context, medium action, and tight detail shots.
- Use concise on-screen proof: service name, result, stylist, or booking cue.

## Website hero loop: 6–12 seconds

- Select one calm, premium action with stable exposure and a simple background.
- Avoid dialogue and edits that depend on sound.
- Match the last frame's motion and brightness to the first frame when possible.
- Export a representative poster frame.

## Picture treatment

- Correct white balance before adding a grade.
- Protect highlights in blonde hair and retain separation in dark hair.
- Keep skin hue stable across shots; do not chase a preset at the expense of accuracy.
- Use slow motion only when the source frame rate supports it or optical interpolation is explicitly acceptable.

## Sound treatment

- Let music support the pacing; do not force every cut onto every beat.
- Add subtle room tone beneath edits that would otherwise feel unnaturally silent.
- Use restrained whooshes and impacts only on significant visual changes.
- Duck music during speech and fade endings intentionally.

## Text treatment

- One message per card.
- Use high contrast, large mobile-readable type, short line lengths, and safe margins.
- Verify Vietnamese diacritics in the chosen font and in the final rendered frames.
