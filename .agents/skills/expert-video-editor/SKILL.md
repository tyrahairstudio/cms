# Expert Video Editor

Treat the edit as a communication piece, not a collection of effects. Build one clear story, then polish picture and sound around it.

## End-to-end workflow

1. Establish the deliverable from context: objective, audience, platform, aspect ratio, duration, language, CTA, and whether audio will autoplay. Use reasonable project defaults when these are not decision-critical.
2. Inventory and inspect every source. Record duration, orientation, usable moments, technical problems, audio value, and continuity. Do not assume filenames describe content accurately.
3. Write a short edit plan or edit decision list before rendering. Include time ranges, shot purpose, on-screen text, transitions, and audio cues.
4. Assemble the rough cut for story and rhythm. Remove repetition, weak starts, dead air, accidental camera movement, and shots that do not advance the message.
5. Add finishing in this order: reframing/stabilization, timing and speed, color correction, restrained grade, transitions/effects, typography/captions, audio mix, then export.
6. Render to a new output. Inspect the complete result and correct visible or audible defects before delivery.

## Editorial standards

- Make the first 1–2 seconds immediately understandable on social video.
- Prefer motivated cuts, match cuts, clean dissolves, and one signature transition over transition packs.
- Preserve natural skin tone and truthful hair color. Do not use beauty effects that erase strand texture or change identity without explicit approval.
- Keep captions concise, readable on a phone, within safe areas, and synchronized to speech.
- Use effects to direct attention. If an effect competes with the hairstyle, face, or message, reduce or remove it.
- Maintain continuity of movement, eyeline, exposure, and color across adjacent shots.

## Tyra Hair Studio defaults

- Salon reel: 9:16, 15–30 seconds, transformation result visible early, clear before/process/after arc, elegant pacing, and a concise booking CTA.
- Before/after: match framing and exposure; do not deliberately degrade the before image; hold the final result long enough to judge it.
- Website hero: 16:9 or layout-matched crop, 6–12 second seamless-feeling loop, calm motion, no essential information embedded only in the video, and a poster image.
- Use premium beauty/editorial styling: warm-neutral color, clean highlights, detailed hair, minimal graphics, and restrained effects.

## Audio and export

- Use `$video-audio-mixer` when adding or balancing a single music/voice track.
- Keep speech intelligible, use music ducking under voice, add short fades, and check for clipping or abrupt endings.
- Export a high-quality master before making platform-specific versions.
- For website delivery, use MP4/H.264/AAC, `yuv420p`, even dimensions, and fast-start metadata. Provide alternate aspect ratios only when requested or clearly required.

## Quality control

Watch the final export from beginning to end. Verify story clarity, spelling, logo placement, safe margins, flash frames, black frames, jump cuts, dropped frames, color consistency, audio sync, loudness, duration, codec, dimensions, and file size. Report the final absolute paths and key export settings.
