Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Your choice comes first. Meta Pixel and the Google advertising tag load only after you allow optional analytics and advertising cookies. Declining does not affect your ability to browse services, view our location or Facebook page preview, call the studio, or open our booking provider.

Information we process

  • Basic website activity such as the page viewed and whether an appointment, phone, or email link was clicked.
  • Technical information commonly provided by a browser, such as device/browser type, approximate location derived by the advertising platform, IP address, and advertising identifiers when optional cookies are allowed.
  • Information you choose to send directly by email or phone. Booking information is collected on the separate GoCheckIn booking service under its own privacy practices.

How we use information

We use website activity to understand which pages and advertisements help people discover Tyra Hair Studio, measure appointment-link and contact-link clicks, prevent duplicate or misleading reporting, and improve our website and advertising.

Advertising and third parties

When you allow optional cookies, website activity may be shared with Meta and Google for measurement, attribution, audience creation, and targeted advertising. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, booking details, hair history, or message contents through our website tracking events.

The embedded Google Map and Facebook page preview load by default as visible website features. Loading or interacting with these embeds may share technical browser or network information with Google or Meta under their respective privacy policies. This is separate from Tyra Hair Studio's optional advertising measurement tags.

Your choices and requests

You can allow or decline optional cookies at any time. You may also ask what information Tyra Hair Studio holds about you, request correction or deletion, or opt out of targeted advertising processing by contacting us. We will review and respond to verifiable requests as required by applicable law.
