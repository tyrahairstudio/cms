Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Your choice comes first. The Meta Pixel and Google Ads measurement tag load only after you allow optional analytics and advertising cookies. Our Google Map and Facebook page preview are visible website features and load independently. Declining optional cookies does not prevent you from browsing services, calling the studio, viewing the map, or opening our booking provider.

Information we process

  • Website activity, such as pages viewed and appointment, phone, or email links clicked, when optional measurement is allowed.
  • Technical information commonly provided to online services, such as IP address, browser and device type, approximate location, referral information, and advertising identifiers.
  • Information you choose to send directly by email or phone. Booking information is collected by the separate GoCheckIn booking service under its own privacy practices.

How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the website, respond when you contact the studio, protect the site from misuse, and—when optional measurement is allowed—understand which pages and advertisements lead people to appointment and contact links.

Advertising, booking, and embedded services

When you allow optional cookies, website activity may be shared with Meta and Google for advertising measurement, attribution, audience creation, and targeted advertising. Tyra Hair Studio does not intentionally include your name, email address, phone number, booking details, hair history, or message contents in website tracking events.

The embedded Google Map and Facebook page preview load by default as visible website features. Loading or interacting with them may share browser, device, or network information with Google or Meta. If you open the booking link, you leave this website and GoCheckIn processes your booking under its own privacy practices.

Your choices and requests

You can allow or decline optional cookies at any time. Where applicable, you may also ask whether Tyra Hair Studio processes your personal information, request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising processing. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law. If we deny a request, you may reply to our decision and ask us to review it.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, to maintain business records, or to meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.
